How the Priority System Works

Tier 1
Emergency — Severe pain, trauma, or infection. These cases are reviewed first.
Tier 2
In Pain — Ongoing discomfort that affects daily life. Reviewed after emergencies.
Tier 3
Routine — Preventive care, checkups, cleaning. First-come, first-served within this tier.

Within each tier, earlier applications get higher priority. If slots fill up, you're placed on a waitlist and automatically promoted when a spot opens.
